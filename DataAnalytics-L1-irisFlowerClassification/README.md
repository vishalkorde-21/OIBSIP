# Retail Sales Analysis (OIBSIP)

## Objective
Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify customer purchasing patterns using Python.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook (VS Code)

## Dataset
Retail Sales Dataset (1000 customer records)

## Analysis Performed
- Customer Gender Distribution
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Gender
- Sales by Age Group
- Sales by Age Group & Gender
- Sales Percentage by Product Category & Gender

## Key Insights
- 46–55 age group generated the highest sales.
- 56–65 age group generated the lowest sales.
- Female customers contributed more in Beauty & Clothing.
- Male customers contributed more in Electronics.
- Overall sales were balanced across genders.
